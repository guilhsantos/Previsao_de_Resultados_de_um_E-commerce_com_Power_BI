# E-commerce sales and customer dashboard (Power BI)

A Power BI dashboard showing how the e-commerce store is selling and who its customers are, to project revenue and recommend actions that improve results.

| Purchase view | Customer view |
|---|---|
| ![Sales](Print_Vendas.png) | ![Customers](Print_Clientes.png) |

## Problem

Sales and customer records were in separate datasets, so simple questions had no answer: how much do we sell per month, in which category, to whom, and through which channel.

## Solution

I related the sales and customer tables in Power BI and built two pages with a layout designed in Figma.

- **Cards:** number of sales, revenue with and without shipping, number of customers, and average income.
- **Lines:** sales count and value per month.
- **Bars:** sales by category, age range, and income range.
- **Filters:** brand, state, channel, department, age, income, and state of birth.

## Result

A view of revenue and customer profile for any slice, which supported the recommendations presented in `Desafio1_GS.pdf`. A challenge from the DNC Data Scientist program, completed with a perfect score.

## How to open

Download `Desafio1_GS.pbix` and open it in Power BI Desktop. The data is in `Base de dados desafio.xlsx`.
